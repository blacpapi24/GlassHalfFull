# Glass Half Full

A splitting duel for two phones. Each round you both get the same thing (a baguette, a fish, a pencil, a kebab, a glass of lemonade…) with a random but realistic size and weight. Cut it into two halves of the same weight or length, or pour the glass exactly half full. Whoever is closer to a perfect half hits the other for the gap between your scores. You each have a set number of hearts, and the hits get bigger from round 3.

- A perfect half scores 100, 1% off about 85, 5% off about 37. A cut within 0.5% adds a 10 point bonus.
- **Two phones:** one of you starts a room and the other joins with its four-letter code (PeerJS). The room is saved on both phones, so a reload or a locked screen picks up where you left off.
- **Pass and play** and **Practice** work on one phone.

Play it at https://blacpapi24.github.io/GlassHalfFull/ (GitHub Pages, `main` branch, root). It's also part of [Dapple](https://blacpapi24.github.io/Dapple/), and rooms work between the two copies.

`index.html` is the whole game. `vendor/peerjs.min.js` is PeerJS (MIT, see `vendor/LICENSE-peerjs`).
